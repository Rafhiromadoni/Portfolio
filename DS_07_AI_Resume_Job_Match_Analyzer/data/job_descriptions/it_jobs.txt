### ROLE: Data Analyst
CATEGORY: IT & Data
DESCRIPTION:
We are looking for a Data Analyst who can collect, clean, validate, analyze, and visualize business data to support operational and strategic decision-making. The candidate will work closely with business users, management, and technical teams to understand reporting needs, define KPIs, identify trends, and translate raw data into actionable insights.

Responsibilities include extracting data from databases, performing data cleaning and transformation, building dashboards, creating recurring reports, analyzing performance metrics, identifying anomalies, preparing presentation materials, and communicating insights to stakeholders. The role also requires the ability to explain data findings clearly to both technical and non-technical audiences.

Required skills include SQL, Python, Excel, Tableau, Power BI, data cleaning, data visualization, exploratory data analysis, statistical analysis, dashboard development, KPI monitoring, reporting, business intelligence, data quality checking, and stakeholder communication.

Preferred skills include ETL, automation reporting, basic data modeling, business process understanding, data storytelling, and experience working with operational, sales, finance, customer, or performance data.
